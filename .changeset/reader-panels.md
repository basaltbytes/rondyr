Fold a message's Classification and Deliveries at the foot of the reader. Each folded row shows the verdict or the delivery statuses; one panel opens at a time, grows upward with a shadow over the message, and stays open from one message to the next, while the message scrolls in the room above it. The refresh button of the deliveries has a tooltip. On a wide screen the page no longer bounces past the app when a pane reaches its end, and the classification table no longer scrolls sideways by a few pixels after a test.
